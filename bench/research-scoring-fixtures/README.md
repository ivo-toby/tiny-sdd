# Research scoring preparation fixtures

These five small fixture pairs are synthetic preparation material for issue
#35. Every pair is explicitly `proposed-unreviewed-human`; the gold manifest is
an example of an intended interface selection, not a reviewed label. They are
not consumed by `benchmark-runner`, and they do not establish a research
adapter, a calibrated score, or a TinyThreshold.

Each directory contains a tiny project under `project/`, a change proposal,
and a proposed gold context manifest. Human review must replace the status and
confirm the cited interfaces before these examples can support research
measurements.
